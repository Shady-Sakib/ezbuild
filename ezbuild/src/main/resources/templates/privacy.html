Privacy

EZBuild stores your account details, delivery addresses, orders, saved builds and assistant chats so the store works across your devices.

Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Card and mobile-wallet details are entered only on SSLCommerz's or Stripe's hosted pages; they never reach our servers.

Messages you send to the AI assistant are processed by Google Gemini to understand your request. We do not send your email, phone or address to the AI provider.

Payments are processed by SSLCommerz and Stripe on their own secure pages; card details never reach our servers.
